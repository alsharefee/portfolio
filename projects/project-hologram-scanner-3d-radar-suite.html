Hologram Scanner & 3D Radar Suite: LiDAR, Pulse Scanner & Holo-Twin

Tired of flat 2D minimaps that break immersion? Hologram Scanner & 3D Radar Suite creates real 3D holograms right inside your game world. Place them inside vehicle cockpits, wear them on a character’s wrist, project them onto command tables, or scan entire landscapes with an expanding pulse wave.

100% Plug & Play: Drag and drop any of the included Blueprints into your level or character—it works immediately with zero code or complex setup. Designed from the ground up for sci-fi cockpits, mech combat, space simulators, and open-world games.

⚡ Performance & Low-Overhead Architecture

Built in native C++ for high-performance projects where every single millisecond counts:

  • Sub-Millisecond CPU Time: Efficient C++ ray traces and sweep math minimize game-thread execution time.
  • Zero GC Hitching: Pre-allocated circular ring buffers ensure 0 new memory allocations per frame, eliminating micro-stutters and garbage collection spikes.
  • 1 Draw Call GPU Instancing: Thousands of holographic blip dots render seamlessly in a single GPU draw call.
  • GPU-Driven Dot Decay: Dot lifetimes and smooth fading curves are computed on the GPU inside the material, keeping CPU threads entirely free.
  • ~80% CPU Savings on Vehicle Twins: Replaces heavy Animation Blueprints with direct bone sampling, forced low LODs, and an authentic sci-fi low FPS update rate.
  • VR, PC & Console Ready: Ultra-lightweight footprint leaves maximum headroom for VR headsets and demanding graphical scenes.

🎮 Three Complete Gameplay Systems

1. 📡 Volumetric 3D Holographic Radar (ARadarHologram)

Scans floors, walls, ceilings, and dynamic obstacles into a miniature 3D hologram in real time. Perfect for tactical command tables and cockpit displays.

  • Real 3D Scanning: Detects full three-dimensional geometry around the player or vehicle.
  • Full Room Coverage: Equal-Distance mode spaces dots evenly across floors, roofs, walls, and airborne drones.
  • Sci-Fi Micro-Cube Voxels: Sharp digital cube blips deliver a crisp, futuristic aesthetic.
  • Sweep Beam & Audio: 360° sweeping beam flashes red on enemy contact with procedural MetaSound audio pings.
  • Target Tracking: Distinguishes terrain from hostiles and hovers floating 3D distance tags above active targets.

2. 🌐 1:1 Ground Pulse Terrain Scanner (AHologramPulseScanner)

Sends an expansive impulse wavefront across uneven ground up to 50m+ in an organic honeycomb pattern to reveal terrain contours and hidden threats.

  • Expanding Wavefront: Organic honeycomb pulse sweeping outward across landscape polygons.
  • Live Slope Colors: Evaluates ground steepness in real time to guide player traversal and navigation:
    • 🟢 Safe Ground (< 25°): Neon Cyan (Easy walking)
    • 🟡 Caution Slope (25°–45°): Amber (Steep climb)
    • 🔴 Hazard Wall (≥ 45°): Danger Red (Sheer cliff / impassable wall)
  • Zero-Clipping Placement: Markers intelligently elevate above ground geometry so they never sink into terrain or flicker.
  • Enemy Tagging: Highlights concealed enemies with 3D floating markers and real-time distance measurements.

3. 🤖 Vehicle & Mech Hologram Twin (UVehicleHologramComponent)

Creates a live holographic mini-twin of your vehicle or mech right inside the cockpit, mirroring movements, steering inputs, articulation, and thrusters in real time.

  • Live Animated Hologram: 3D mini-hologram mirroring steering wheels, mech legs, or ship thrusters in sync with the real vehicle.
  • Authentic Sci-Fi Stutter: Updates at a controllable low FPS for a classic holographic aesthetic while saving ~80% CPU cycles over Animation Blueprints.
  • Rotating Display: Optional turntable spin for UI menu screens, vehicle selection, or cockpit consoles.

📦 Ready-to-Use Drag-and-Drop Blueprints

  • BP_Radar_VerySmall (15 cm): Personal wrist gadgets, wearable pip-boys, handheld scanners.
  • BP_Radar_Small (50 cm): Fighter jet cockpits, mech consoles, rover dashboards.
  • BP_Radar_Medium (1.2 m): Tactical command briefing tables, starship bridge maps.
  • BP_Radar_Large (3.5 m): Base perimeter defense, planetary radar installations.
  • BP_Radar_1to1_Environment (1:1 Scale): Full-scale holographic arena LiDAR sweeps.
  • BP_HologramPulseScanner (50m+ Wave): Open-world terrain scanning & slope hazard detection.
  • BP_HoloShowroomCharacter (Playable Pawn): First-person character with wrist radar ([Q]) and pulse scanner ([F]) pre-attached.

🛠️ Easy Customization (Details Panel)

Customize almost everything directly in the Unreal Editor with simple checkboxes and sliders:

  • Checkboxes: Toggle Enemy 3D Text, Range Readout, Target Counter, and Equal Distance Dots.
  • Visuals: Choose cube or sphere dots, adjust dot scale, and pick custom beam and alert colors.
