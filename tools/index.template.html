Recognition

🏆 Rockstars Impact Award · Sports-Tech

AFC Ajax: Real-Time 3D Tracking & VR Simulation

AFC Ajax & Team Rockstar IT Mar 2026 ~200 devs · 24 teams Johan Cruyff ArenA

Awarded 1st place Rockstars Impact Award at the official AFC Ajax Hackathon—competing against ~200 top developers across 24 teams at the Johan Cruyff ArenA—for engineering an agile real-time 3D sports data visualization engine in Unity. While conventional analytics teams built 2D charts, our team ingested Ajax’s official 21-point skeletal tracking dataset and translated raw athlete telemetry into an interactive, first-person Virtual Reality match simulation.

Enterprise sports tech platforms have historically required multi-million-dollar broadcast pipelines to turn player tracking feeds into 3D simulations. When presented with Ajax’s raw high-frequency tracking data—capturing micro-movements across 21 joint points per player—I architected a custom C# engine in Unity that parsed the telemetry streams into rigged 3D player avatars with sub-frame accuracy in real time.

What We Built · Tactical VR Match Intelligence

We put the judges and coaches directly inside the VR headset onto the pitch. Rather than studying static tactical heatmaps on flat monitors, they experienced the match through the eyes of an Ajax player or referee—inspecting passing lanes, defensive spacing, and off-the-ball movement in full 3D spatial fidelity.
