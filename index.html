Recognition

Awards, hackathons & industry recognition

🏆 Rockstars Impact Award

Ajax × Team Rockstar IT Hackathon

AFC Ajax & Team Rockstar ITMar 2026~200 devs · 24 teams

I'm incredibly proud to announce that my team Sam Jager, Dante van der Pijl, Julian Bakker, Vladislav Manolov and I took home the Rockstars Impact Award at the AFC Ajax Hackathon this weekend, beating out ~200 talented developers across 24 teams! ⚽️💻

When I read that we will be presented with Ajax's dataset, a detailed 21-point skeletal tracking system of player limb movements, I thought about how cool it would be to use Realtime 3D and Unity to make a Virtual Reality sim experience. So, I packed up my VR headset and headed to the hackathon.

What we built

While most teams focused on 2D analytics dashboards, we wanted to build an immersive spatial simulation. Leveraging my experience in C# and XR development, I built a Unity VR engine that reads the complex 21-point tracking data and translates it into a realtime, first-person Virtual Reality environment. We put the judges in a headset and took them on a tour of the match directly through the eyes of an Ajax player, allowing them to see the exact situation from the player's or referee's perspective.
